## 1. When a spreadsheet should become a bespoke business app

**Primary query:** when to build a custom business app vs spreadsheet  
**Answer-first:** Build a bespoke app when more than one person depends on the sheet, when status lives in email, or when the same data is retyped into a CRM. If only you edit it and nothing breaks when you are away, keep the spreadsheet.

Outline: failure modes · decision checklist · smallest useful app · SmartFunction approach link.

## 2. What AI automation is actually worth doing in a UK SME this quarter

**Primary query:** AI automation for small business UK  
**Answer-first:** Automate high-volume, low-judgement work with clear inputs/outputs — enquiry triage, follow-up reminders, data entry between tools. Do not start with “an AI agent for everything.”

Outline: ROI filter · three safe first automations · where humans stay in the loop · Playground CTA.

## 3. How to map a broken sales follow-up process before you buy more tools

**Primary query:** sales follow-up process mapping  
**Answer-first:** Write the steps as they happen today (not as the CRM says they should). Circle every handoff that relies on memory. Fix those handoffs before adding another prospecting licence.

Outline: box-and-arrow method · Playground walkthrough · example from Work page (honest, illustrative).

## 4. Bespoke app vs off-the-shelf SaaS: a practical decision guide

**Primary query:** custom software vs SaaS for small business  
**Answer-first:** Buy SaaS when your process is common and you can adapt. Build when the process is the advantage — or when you are paying for five tools that still need a spreadsheet to connect them.

## 5. FAQ: how long custom business software takes when you ship the smallest useful version first

**Primary query:** how long does bespoke software take  
**Answer-first:** Expect a useful first version in weeks when scope is ruthlessly cut to one bottleneck — not a nine-month rebuild of the whole company.
