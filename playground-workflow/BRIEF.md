# Playground brief (stable)

Keep every iteration under this brief. Do not expand the product into a generic diagram tool or a sales form with a canvas bolted on.

## Who it is for
UK operators and founders who know their process is messy (email, spreadsheets, memory, CRM retyping) and want a concrete next step — not a demo for its own sake.

## What success looks like
| For the visitor | For SmartFunction |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Sees their real workflow reflected | Receives steps, connections, kinds, and descriptions that reveal bottlenecks |
| Understands the free analysis trade | Can reply with a scoped solution/service path |
| Leaves with a clearer “before → better” picture | Has consent + contact context when they ask for a call or project |

## Narrative spine (keep intact)
1. **Pick a process** — templates that sound like real businesses, or blank.
2. **Arrange your steps** — drag, connect, rename, type (manual / automated / approval / decision).
3. **Make it smarter** — one free Smart Builder analysis; no invented savings or guarantees.
4. **Turn it into a project** — talk it through *or* send diagrams + analysis; neither commits them.

## Copy principles
- Plain English. Name the pain (memory, retyping, email threads) before the tool.
- Explain the value exchange before asking for email.
- Never promise automatic savings, timelines, or “AI will fix it.”
- SmartFunction stays founder-led and honest: a person reads the workflow before replying.

## Anti-goals
- Arcade / toy framing that undercuts a B2B discovery tool.
- Extra steps that collect vanity fields before the visitor has mapped anything useful.
- Redesigning the whole SmartFunction brand on this page.
- Shipping “more features” when the real gap is orientation, connection discoverability, or CTA clarity.
